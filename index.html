01 / Explore

01The journey

Follow one customer.
Find the friction.

We pick one real customer and walk their route from first message to filed record. Each stop gets the same three questions.

use ↑ ↓ to step through

Diagnostic / stop 01 of 04Example audit

A · What we review

  • Where new enquiries arrive
  • Who replies, and how quickly
  • What gets asked every single time

B · Typical friction

Enquiries buried in DMs

Messages split across Instagram, email and WhatsApp. Some get missed, others answered twice.

C · A sensible improvement

A single place for incoming requests

One short form or inbox that captures the basics up front, so the first reply can be a useful one.

Common patterns we look for, not findings about your business. Your map is based on your call.

02 / Listen

02The approach

45 minutes.
One call.

A video call with screen-sharing if it helps. Bring the tools you actually use, even the messy spreadsheet. Especially the messy spreadsheet.

no prep needed, honestly →

  1. 00:00

    How things run today

    You talk, we listen. Which tools, which people, which bits feel heavier than they should.

  2. 10:00

    One customer, start to finish

    We pick a recent customer and trace them through inquiry, booking, payment and records.

  3. 30:00

    Where it slows down

    We point out the handoffs and repeats as we hear them, so nothing is a surprise later.

  4. 40:00

    What happens next

    We agree what goes into your map. No proposal, no pitch deck at the end.
