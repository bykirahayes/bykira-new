Cookies and storage

No advertising tracking or analytics has been installed in the ByKira application code.

Publication and verification status

This page reflects the current GitHub-hosted website, but verified legal business details and a full operational compliance review are still outstanding. It is not legal certification.

Current application behaviour

The current frontend contains no Google Analytics, advertising pixels, consent-management vendor, localStorage or sessionStorage persistence, or app-set marketing cookies. The optional pause-animations control is temporary for the current page session and is not stored on your device.

Website delivery

GitHub Pages serves the site, and the hosting infrastructure may process technical connection or security information. Hosting-level cookies and storage have not yet been confirmed by live browser/network testing.

Why there is no cookie banner

A banner is not automatically required where no consent-requiring storage or access technology is used. UK PECR includes exceptions, and technologies needing consent must be assessed before implementation. A decorative consent banner is not a substitute for an actual cookie audit.

If tracking is added

Before analytics, advertising, non-essential storage or similar technology is introduced, the operator must assess ICO guidance, update this page, and implement meaningful choices/consent or an applicable exception as required. See ICO storage and access guidance (opens in a new tab).
