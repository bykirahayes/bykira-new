Accessibility

We're designing ByKira to work with keyboard navigation, readable contrast and reduced motion.

Publication and verification status

This page reflects the current GitHub-hosted website, but verified legal business details and a full operational compliance review are still outstanding. It is not legal certification.

What has been implemented

The site includes a skip-to-content link, visible keyboard focus, semantic sections, named form fields and error messages, keyboard-operable journey steps, accessible dialogs and a pause/resume control for decorative animation. System-level reduced-motion preferences are also respected.

What is not yet certified

A full WCAG 2.2 AA assessment, screen-reader testing across major platforms, complete colour/zoom audits and mobile assisted-technology testing have not been completed. We cannot claim full compliance or universal accessibility at this stage.

Managing animation

Use the pause button in the top navigation on the homepage to stop decorative CSS animations. You can also turn on reduced motion in your device or browser settings. The website will respect that preference.

Reporting accessibility barriers

We still need to publish a direct business email for accessibility feedback and alternative ways to access information. For guidance on web accessibility, see WCAG 2.2 quick reference (opens in a new tab).
