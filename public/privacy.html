Privacy information

What this website currently does with information, and what remains to be verified.

Publication and verification status

This page reflects the current GitHub-hosted website, but verified legal business details and a full operational compliance review are still outstanding. It is not legal certification.

Who operates this website

ByKira is the trading name shown on this website. The legal operator's verified identity, geographical business address and direct contact email have not yet been supplied for publication. This is an outstanding compliance requirement and this notice is interim, not a completed UK GDPR Article 13 notice.

Information entered into the audit form

The audit form runs locally in your browser. It asks for your name, email, business and a short description, but does not transmit these details to ByKira or make a booking. It prepares text you can copy or, once a verified recipient is configured, open in your own email app. Do not enter special-category or confidential personal information.

If you email us

If you later choose to send a message, your email provider and the recipient's provider process the message. The business operator needs to verify its lawful basis (for example steps you request before a contract, where applicable), retention schedule, suppliers, international transfers and processes for responding to rights requests before collecting and processing enquiries. We are not claiming those arrangements exist already.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process IP addresses and browser/connection information to serve and protect the site. The site does not have an application analytics or advertising tracker in its source code. Fonts are served as bundled files from this website rather than fetched from Google Fonts.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable lawful basis, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, and data portability. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). The operator needs to provide a verified direct contact route for requests before this notice is complete.

Questions or complaints

For information about data protection rights and complaints, see the ICO complaints guidance (opens in a new tab). The business contact details must be added before this information can operate as a complete privacy notice.
