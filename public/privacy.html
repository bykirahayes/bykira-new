Privacy information

What this website currently does with information, and what remains to be verified.

Publication and verification status

This page reflects the current GitHub-hosted website, but verified legal business details and a full operational compliance review are still outstanding. It is not legal certification.

Who operates this website

ByKira is the trading name shown on this website. ByKira is being operated as a sole-trader brand, not as a limited company. Contact enquiries and privacy questions at info@bykira.co.uk. The individual's legal name and a geographical business/service address have not yet been supplied for publication. This notice remains interim, not a completed UK GDPR Article 13 notice.

Information entered into the audit form

The audit form runs locally in your browser. It asks for your name, email, business and a short description, but does not transmit these details to ByKira or make a booking. It prepares text you can copy, or open in your email app addressed to info@bykira.co.uk. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the draft from your email app. Do not enter special-category or confidential personal information.

If you email us

If you choose to send an email to info@bykira.co.uk, your email provider and the recipient's email provider process its contents and delivery metadata. The intended purpose is to read your enquiry and reply about services you asked about; there is no newsletter sign-up or marketing consent in this form. The sole trader still needs to document and confirm the appropriate lawful basis (such as pre-contractual steps where appropriate), email provider arrangements, international transfers and a retention/deletion policy. We do not invent those arrangements or a retention period.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process IP addresses and browser/connection information to serve and protect the site. The site does not have an application analytics or advertising tracker in its source code. Fonts are served as bundled files from this website rather than fetched from Google Fonts.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable lawful basis, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, and data portability. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Contact info@bykira.co.uk to raise a privacy request. The operator's legal identity, processing details and response procedures still need completing before this is a full privacy notice.

Questions or complaints

For information about data protection rights and complaints, see the ICO complaints guidance (opens in a new tab). For questions about information you send, use info@bykira.co.uk. The operator's legal name, geographical address, processor information and retention policy remain outstanding.
